Tic Tac Chaos: privacy policy

DRAFT — for review · 26 October 2026

Tic Tac Chaos is a free game made by JarHead Labs. You can play on your own phone without an account. An account is only needed to play strangers online (Find Match), to host a friend game and to keep your records and game history. This page says what the game keeps, why, for how long, and how to delete it.

In short

  • No ads, no trackers, nothing sold. The game keeps only what it needs to run.
  • With an account: your email, your username, your game records and history.
  • Without one: a random device id and app events, for playtest stats, and the log of a match you report.
  • You can delete your account, and everything kept under it, from inside the game at any time.

What we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Your email addressWhen you log in with an email and password, Google or AppleTo sign you in, to prove the address (a verification email), to reset a password, and to answer a request about your data. It is never shown to other players.
Your usernameWhen you pick oneHow other players see you: in matches, in their game history and in their Top opponents.
Game records and historyEvery online match (Find Match and friend games), and local matches you play while logged inYour wins, losses and draws, the squares won and lost per mini-game, and the final board of each match, for your stats and game history. Other players see your username and records; your history and opponents stay yours.
Top opponentsEvery online match against another player with a usernameYour record against each opponent, shown on your own stats page.
A random device id and app eventsEvery time the game opens, starts or ends a matchPlaytest stats: how many people play, which mini-games get played and finished, and whether the game works on their phones. The id is made up by your phone (never a hardware id) and stored hashed. Logged in, these events also move your account's "last played" time.
Match logsOnline matches, and a match you report with "Report a problem"A compact event log of the match (moves, timings, errors, your note) so problems can be found and fixed.
A tester tagWhen you open the game from a playtest link (for example ?t=sam)So the playtest stats can be grouped by who was invited. The tag is whatever the link says; use a nickname.

Nothing else is collected: no contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, no analytics from third parties.

How long we keep it

  • Account data (email, username, records, history, Top opponents, local records): until you delete the account.
  • Match logs: 30 days after the match's last event.
  • Device events, the device id, an account's "last played" time, and the alert log (a note that someone was waiting for a match): 90 days. A device not seen for 90 days is forgotten with its events.
  • Finished online matches: the live record is deleted a day after the match; the result stays in each player's history.
  • Anonymous counts (matches played per day, per mini-game, logins): kept, with nothing in them about anyone.

Deleting your account

Open your stats from the main menu (tap your username), scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm. The game deletes your profile, username, records, game history, Top opponents and local records, and the login itself, at once. The username becomes free for someone else. Other players keep their own history of matches against you (the result and the name you played under at the time). A match in progress must be finished or left first.

If you cannot get into the game, email privacy@tictacchaos.com from the account's address and we will delete it for you.

Who else sees it

The game runs on Firebase (Google LLC), which stores the data and handles sign-in, as our processor. Firebase's own terms and privacy notice are at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Signing in with Google or Apple shares your email with the game as those providers describe. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and there are no advertising or analytics companies in the game.

The Trivia mini-game's questions come from the Open Trivia Database (CC BY-SA 4.0), included in the game: no request goes to them while you play.

Children

Accounts are for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly keep an account for anyone younger; if you believe a child under 13 has made one, email us and we will delete it. The game itself can be played without an account.

Security

Every online match is run by the server, which checks every move; nothing hidden in a game is sent to a phone before it is revealed. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and never seen by us. Your email is read by the server only to sign you in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a change that matters is announced in the game.
