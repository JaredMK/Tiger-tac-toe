Tic Tac Chaos: privacy

Updated 27 October 2026.

What we collect

  • Your account email.
  • Your username.
  • Your gameplay and match data: results, records and game history.
  • An anonymous device ID, made up by your phone.

Why

To run the game, to match you with other players, and to find and fix bugs. We do not sell your data or show ads.

Where it is stored

With Google Firebase.

How long we keep it

Account, record and history data: until you delete your account. Match logs: 30 days. Device ID and app events: 90 days.

Delete your account

In the game: open your stats from the main menu, scroll down and tap Delete account. Or email us from your account's address and we will delete it.

Children

Tic Tac Chaos is not for children under 13.
