Tic Tac Chaos: fun review mock-ups

Companion to docs/fun-review.md. Fourteen phone-size screens (360 × 720) in the Comic Chaos style: the recommended first-time experience for a reworked Super Chaos, Classic's first match, and four key moments. Static pictures with a little motion; nothing here is wired to the game.

Tappable A skewed panel with an ink outline and a hard shadow, as in the live game.
Information Flat: speech bubbles, caption boxes, bursts. Never skewed or shadowed.
New in these mock-ups The rival's ghost and "rival cam", the pick countdown, the clash duel, the round card, coach bubbles, match-point glow, the passport and the Daily Board.

A · Super Chaos, reworked: the first 25 seconds

A scripted intro round the first time someone plays: three beats, one tap each, about 25 seconds. The same three beats are every round afterwards: Pick (secret, together), Reveal (split or clash), Play (both at once, the rival visible). See Part 1 of the review.

B · Classic: the first match

A first-time visitor taps one button and is playing: a starter board against the computer on Easy, the player moving first, with the existing "?" card shown once the first time each mini-game is opened. See Part 2, Onboarding.

C · Key moments

Four small additions that give matches a shape: a visible match point, a Chaos Round inside Classic, a Daily Board to come back for, and a series score that makes "Rematch" mean something.
