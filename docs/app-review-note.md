Tic Tac Chaos is tic-tac-toe where every square is a mini-game: win the mini-game to claim the square, three squares in
a row win the match.

**Signing in is optional.** Everything offline works without an account: Play Computer (from the main menu) and Play a
Friend → Same phone. An account is used only for online play with a friend and to keep stats.

**Demo account** (email login, already verified, with a username):
- Email: [demo account email]
- Password: [demo account password]

Log in from the main menu's Log in button. To try online play with one device, open Play a Friend → Host game on one
device or simulator and Join game with the code on another; or play the computer, which needs no second device.
Continue with Google and Continue with Apple are both offered on the login screen. An account can be deleted in the app:
Settings → Delete account (an Apple sign-in account is asked to confirm with Apple, and its Sign in with Apple token is
revoked).

**About the luck mini-games.** Some mini-games use luck themes (a small number wheel, dice, a coin flip, a peg board, a
card game of War, a claw machine). They are only ways to win a square of tic-tac-toe:

- There is **no real money** and no wagering of anything: no in-app purchases, no virtual currency, no chips or credits.
- There are **no prizes**: nothing is won, bought, exchanged or carried out of the game; a win claims a square on the board.
- There is **no payout language**: the wheel game's players pick numbers and see "Hit" or "Miss"; nothing is bet.
- There are **no ads** and no links to gambling services.

We answered the age rating questionnaire's Simulated Gambling question as Infrequent/Mild on that basis.
