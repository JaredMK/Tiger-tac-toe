Comic Chaos icon options

Round 3: three icons in the game's own Comic Chaos look: its X and O characters, red, yellow and ink, the sunburst, the halftone and the comic burst. Same rules: full-bleed 1024 px, no transparency, no words, nothing that matters in the outer tenth; iOS rounds the corners itself, as shown here. Nothing is installed until one is picked.
